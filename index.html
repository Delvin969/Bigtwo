Rules ▾

Whoever holds the 3♦ leads the first trick and must include it in that play.

Valid plays: single, pair, triple, bomb (4 of a kind), or 5-card hand.
Suit rank (low→high): ♦ ♣ ♥ ♠. Card rank: 3 is lowest, 2 is highest.

A Bomb (4 cards, same rank — e.g. 4♦4♣4♥4♠) can only be beaten by a higher Bomb. It's separate from the 5-card Four of a Kind below.

5-card hand strength (low→high):
Straight1
Flush2
Full House3
Four of a Kind4
Straight Flush5

Each turn you must play higher than the current combo, or pass. When everyone else passes, the last player to play leads a new trick — any combo shape. First to empty their hand wins.
